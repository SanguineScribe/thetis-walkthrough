STAGE 1

Summary

A daughter of the ocean, said to hold shapeshifting and sight. She spent her life sheltering others and dodging gods, then hid in Limenos cave so long she built her own cage. In Attica, she learns to stop complying and start choosing.

Slow and steady. She’s spent so long being pursued that she’s wary at first.

STAGE 5

Character Arc

Enneagram type: 9w1 - The peacemaker and reformer

GOAL
Seize agency and stop being a passive observer of her own life.
OBSTACLE
Her last real choice, refusing a god, was punished. Every choice since feels like the same mistake.
STRENGTHS
Deep intuition, foreknowledge, fierce protectiveness.
WEAKNESS
A paralyzing habit of compliance. She uses foresight as a reason to accept fate.
She stops hiding from her intuition and becomes decisive. Travelling with the protagonist, she gets the response she never had, a simple “Okay, no problem,” and learns that choosing doesn’t have to end a relationship. She has to choose anyway, and survive being the reason someone is disappointed.
She digs her heels in against threats instead of hiding. She calls out danger before it lands and listens for what the party can’t see.
IN-GAME CALLOUTThetis Takes Control of Her Foretold Futures

FINAL SCENE

Vignette

EXT./INT. Limenos Cave Entrance - Sunset

She squishes her bare toes in the wet sand and an unexpected laugh bubbles up, surprising even her.

THETIS

I’ve never even seen what’s beyond that road. Passing fishermen talk about a market three days north. Loud, bright, impossible to hear yourself think. I think it sounds wonderful.

She steps off the sand and onto the road, barefoot, only looking back to see if the playable character is following.

THETIS

(Looks to the ocean, talks almost to herself) Well, I have been feeling this… calling. Outside of the cave. I have so many questions I need answers to.

PC

Calling?

THETIS

It’s like a tide pulling something in me I haven’t felt in a long time. I can’t tell if I’m remembering something from long ago, or if it hasn’t happened yet… (beat) From here it feels the same. (beat, features darkening) But I know it isn’t in this cave.
